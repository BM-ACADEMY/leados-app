It is slow, it gets forgotten, and it does not scale. AllianceOS makes this happen automatically and safely, every single day.\nWhat it does, in one picture\nAllianceOS is the "hunter" at the top of the ABM flywheel. It finds and warms up B2B prospects, then hands the interested ones to a human to close.\n   Prospect lists (colleges, companies, shops, coordinators)\n                      |\n                      v\n   +----------------------------------------------+\n   |            A L L I A N C E O S               |\n   |                                              |\n   |   Picks channel  ->  Email  or  WhatsApp     |\n   |   Personalises each message (AI)             |\n   |   Sends + follows up 4 times                 |\n   |   Stops the moment someone replies           |\n   +----------------------------------------------+\n                      |  (a reply comes in)\n                      v\n   +----------------------------------------------+\n   |   LeadOS Inbox  ->  Kamar / Karthika close   |\n   +----------------------------------------------+\nThe four audiences it targets\nAudience\nBest channel\nWhat we offer them\nCollege principals / TPOs\nEmail\nTraining + placement partnership (MoU) via BM Academy\nCompany HR / corporates\nEmail\nReady trained candidates via CoreTalents\nLocal clinics / shops / SMBs\nEmail → WhatsApp on opt-in\nLeads, visibility, and marketing via BM TechX\nIV trip coordinators\nEmail\nGroup student trips via TravellersNeed\nWhy two channels — and why email leads\nEmail is the cold channel for everyone. \nIt is safe to send cold (when properly authenticated). \nWhatsApp is used only for people who opt in\n — via a QR code, a click-to-WhatsApp ad, or by replying to us first. Cold WhatsApp to a bought list is against Meta policy and gets numbers banned, so AllianceOS never does it.\nPart 2 — What is involved\nThese are all the moving parts of AllianceOS, explained in plain words before we get technical. Think of them as the building blocks.\n1. The prospect list\nA list of businesses/colleges/companies we want to reach — with name, phone, email, industry, and location. Kamar and Karthika supply these lists. AllianceOS cleans them, removes duplicates, and removes anyone on the "do-not-contact" list.\n2. The channels (Email + WhatsApp)\nThe two ways AllianceOS sends messages. Each has its own dedicated, separate setup so they never interfere with our main accounts.\n3. The AI writer & reader\nAI (Grok) does four things: it writes a personalised version of each message, it reads incoming replies to understand if the person is interested, it drafts a suggested reply for a human to approve, and it scores how "warm" each lead is. The AI never sends anything on its own — a human always approves.\n4. The AI Brain (Knowledge Base)\nThe AI’s memory. Instead of hardcoding brand facts and answers into the system, everything the AI is allowed to say lives in one place that Kamar or the OM can edit — no code changes. It holds three things:\nBrand & offer facts \n— what each brand does, prices we can share, and proof points (1400+ trained, 150+ placed, 4.8 star, 750+ businesses), per audience.\nObjection to approved-answer pairs \n— when a prospect says "too costly" or "we already have an agency", the AI replies with your pre-approved answer instead of improvising.\nEditable prompts \n— the tone and rules for each AI job, tuned from a screen so the voice can change without touching a workflow.\nChange a price or a proof point once in the Brain, and every future message and reply updates. AllianceOS pulls its brand facts from the LeadOS AI Brain so there is one source of truth, never two.\n5. The follow-up sequence\nMost people do not reply to the first message. AllianceOS sends up to four messages, spaced out, and stops the moment someone replies:\nTouch\nWhen\nPurpose\n1\nDay 0\nIntroduction + one clear ask\n2\nDay 2\nShort, friendly reminder\n3\nDay 5\nNew angle + proof (1400+ trained, 150+ placed, 4.8 star)\n4\nDay 9\nPolite "break-up" message — this one pulls the most replies\n6. The safety guards\nThe most important part. Cold messaging can get a phone number banned or an email domain marked as spam. These guards prevent that (full detail in Part 3):\nSeparate, isolated numbers and email domain — never our main ones.\nSlow warm-up — new numbers start with very few messages a day and build up.\nA quality monitor that auto-pauses any number showing warning signs.\nA suppression list so we never message someone who said no.\n7. The reply handler & LeadOS connection\nWhen anyone replies, AllianceOS immediately stops messaging them, reads their reply with AI, and pushes it into the LeadOS inbox with a suggested response — so Kamar or Karthika can close the deal. Nothing is ever lost or forgotten.\nWho does what\nPerson\nResponsibility\nDeveloper\nBuilds the whole system, workflows, and safety guards\nKamar / Karthika\nSupply prospect lists, approve AI-drafted replies, close deals\nAllianceOS (the tool)\nSends, follows up, protects the numbers, routes replies\nPart 3 — The technical build\nThis part is for the developer. Breaking any one can ban a number or poison our real email domain.\n1.  \nLeadOS number 99445 09441 is sacred — inbound only. AllianceOS NEVER cold-sends from it.\n2.  \nAllianceOS WhatsApp uses its own number(s), separate from LeadOS. It has four jobs:\nAI job\nInput\nOutput\n1. Personalise message\nProspect row + base template\nOne tailored message (2–4 lines)\n2. Classify reply\nIncoming reply text\nIntent label (interested / question / objection / not-interested)\n3. Draft reply\nReply + prospect context\nSuggested response for HUMAN approval\n4. Score lead\nFull thread + prospect data\nWarmth score 0–100\nGolden rule for AI\nThe AI never auto-sends a reply. It only drafts. Every reply is approved (or edited) by a human in the LeadOS inbox before it goes out. This protects tone and keeps us compliant.\nExample prompt — personalise (Job 1)\nSYSTEM: You write B2B outreach for ABM Groups (Pondicherry).\nTone: warm, direct, Tanglish for SMB / formal for college & HR.\nMax 60 words. One clear CTA. No hype. Use only proof provided.\n \nUSER:\n  Audience: {{audience}}   Touch: {{touch_no}} ({{purpose}})\n  Prospect: {{name}}, {{business_name}}, {{industry}}, {{location}}\n  Base template: {{template_body}}\n  Proof allowed: 1400+ trained, 150+ placed, 4.8 star, 750+ businesses.\n  Return ONLY the final message text.\n3.5 The AI Brain / Knowledge Base\nThe Brain feeds the four AI jobs instead of hardcoding facts in prompts. Three tables plus retrieval steps make it work:\nalliance_kb \n— brand and offer facts, one row per fact, tagged by audience and brand, with an active flag.\nalliance_objections \n— objection text mapped to an approved answer, tagged by audience.\nalliance_prompts \n— the editable system prompt for each job (personalise, classify, draft, score), by channel.\nRetrieval is wired into the workflows: before every personalise call (AL03/AL04) a step fetches the right brand facts + prompt and injects them; in the reply handler (AL07) a step matches the reply to approved objection answers before the AI drafts. A daily sync (AL09) pulls brand facts from the LeadOS AI Brain into alliance_kb so there is a single source of truth.\nWhy this matters\nWithout the Brain, changing one price means editing 8 workflows, and the AI free-styles offers on replies. With it, you change a fact once and every message updates — and replies use your approved words, which is the single biggest jump in reply quality and safety.\n3.6 The 9 n8n workflows\nEach workflow is one job.
