ABM GROUPS\nAllianceOS\nSetup & Onboarding Guide\nAccounts · WhatsApp templates & approval · Cold email domain · AI keys · Webhooks · Auto follow-up\nThe step-by-step account and infrastructure setup — everything to do before the workflows can run.\nVersion 2 · Internal use only\nBefore you start — the big picture\nThe workflows I built are the "engine". This guide is the "fuel and wiring" — the real-world accounts and settings that make them run. Set these up in order. Two rules shape everything:\nTwo rules that decide the whole setup\n1.  Email is the cold channel. \nWhatsApp is NOT for cold blasting — Meta bans numbers that message purchased lists. WhatsApp is used only for people who opt in (QR code, click-to-WhatsApp, or who message us first).\n2.  Nothing is sent from our real accounts. \nCold email uses a separate domain; WhatsApp uses a separate number pool. Our main domain and the LeadOS number are never used for outreach.\nSetup order (do it top to bottom)\nStep\nWhat you set up\nTime\nA\nWhatsApp Cloud API — Meta account, number(s), tokens\n1–3 days (verification)\nB\nWhatsApp templates — submit & get approved\nMinutes–48 hrs each\nC\nCold email domain — buy, authenticate, warm up\n3 weeks warm-up\nD\nAI (Grok) API key\n10 minutes\nE\nn8n credentials & environment variables\n1 hour\nF\nWebhooks — inbound replies & upload\n1 hour\nG\nApproval flow & auto follow-up settings\n30 minutes\nThe long pole is the email warm-up (3 weeks) — start Step C on Day 1 so it warms while you build everything else.\nPart A — WhatsApp Cloud API setup\nMeta shut down the old on-premise API in October 2025, so Cloud API is the only path — which is what we use. Here is the account setup for our isolated number pool.\nA.1 One-time accounts\nCreate (or use) a Meta Business Manager account at business.facebook.com. Complete Business Verification — this can take 1–3 business days, so start it first.\nGo to developers.facebook.com, create an App, and add the "WhatsApp" product to it.\nThis creates a WhatsApp Business Account (WABA) linked to the app.\nA.2 Add your number(s)\nStart with 1 well-warmed number + 1 backup. \nUse fresh, dedicated numbers not already on the normal WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business app. You do NOT need 3 to send more — see the note below.\nIn WhatsApp Manager, add each phone number and verify it by OTP.\nSet the display name and business profile (name, logo, category) for each.\nFor each number, copy three things: \nthe Phone Number ID, the WABA ID, and a permanent access token (create a System User in Business Settings and generate a non-expiring token). These go into the database and n8n.\nWhy a small pool (and why not for volume)\nSince October 2025 the daily limit is shared across all numbers in one Business Portfolio — 3 numbers at the 1,000 tier still send 1,000/day total, not 3,000. So a second/third number is for redundancy (if one goes red, the others keep running) and purpose-separation, not more reach. Start with one, keep one backup, add a third only when campaigns are steady.\nStore these per number\nphone_number_id, waba_id, access_token — one set per number, saved in the alliance_numbers table (tokens encrypted). AL04 reads these to send, and rotates between numbers.\nPart B — WhatsApp templates: submission & approval\nThis is the part most people get wrong. Any message you send to start a conversation (outside a 24-hour window after they message you) must use a template that Meta has pre-approved. Free-form messages only work for 24 hours after the customer messages you first.\nB.1 The three template categories\nCategory\nUse\nApproval\nMarketing\nPromotions, offers — needs opted-in contacts\nStrictest\nUtility\nExpected updates tied to an action they took\nEasiest\nAuthentication\nOTP / login codes only\nFixed format\nThe rule that protects our numbers\nMarketing templates may only go to people who OPTED IN. Sending marketing templates to a purchased or scraped list is the fastest way to get a number banned. So on WhatsApp we do not cold-blast. We bring people in first (see B.4), then message them.\nB.2 How to submit a template\nGo to Meta Business Suite → WhatsApp Manager → Message Templates → Create Template.\nName: \nlowercase letters, numbers, underscores only (e.g. techx_intro_v1).\nCategory & language: \npick the honest category. If you mislabel a promotional message as Utility, Meta reclassifies it to Marketing automatically and charges Marketing rates.\nBody: \nwrite the message. Put variables as {{1}}, {{2}}. A variable cannot be at the very start or very end of the message — that is an automatic rejection.\nSample values: \nyou must provide an example for each {{1}} so the reviewer understands it. Missing samples = rejection.\nSubmit. Status goes Pending → Approved or Rejected, usually within minutes but up to 24–48 hours.\nCommon rejection reasons\nMalformed variables, or a variable at the start/end of the message.\nWrong category (promotional content labelled Utility).\nToo generic, or content that reads like spam / asks for sensitive data.\nAbout half of templates fail on the first try. If rejected, fix and resubmit — a rejected name is fine to reuse after editing; a deleted approved name is locked for 30 days.\nB.3 After approval\nApproved wording is locked. To change it, edit (limited to 10 times per 30 days) or clone a new version.\nIf many people block/report a template, Meta pauses it. Keep content relevant and only send to opted-in people.\nStore the approved template name + variables in the alliance_templates table so AL04 can call it.\nB.4 How we get WhatsApp opt-in (so we stay compliant)\nBecause cold WhatsApp is banned, we make the prospect start the chat or opt in first. Any of these opens a compliant path:\nClick-to-WhatsApp ads \n— a Meta ad with a "Chat on WhatsApp" button. When they tap it, they message us first, opening a free 24-hour window for real conversation.\nQR codes / wa.me links \n— on flyers, GBP, visiting cards. Scanning starts a chat with us.\nA first touch on email \n— the email can invite them to WhatsApp us; when they do, the window opens.\nTwo ways to market on WhatsApp\n1.  Opt-in lane — \nQR / click-to-WhatsApp ads bring warm contacts in. Highest quality, lowest risk.\n2.  Bulk campaign lane — \nupload a list of numbers and send an approved marketing template to all of them (see B.5). Works, but must respect tier limits and quality.\nB.5 Running a bulk WhatsApp campaign from an uploaded list\nThis is the "upload numbers → WhatsApp marketing starts" flow. It works through the same workflows. The one rule: the first message to a cold number must be an approved Marketing template.\nVerify your business first. \nUnverified accounts are capped at 250 messages per 24 hours. After Business Verification you jump to 1,000/day, then climb to 10,000, 100,000, and unlimited as quality stays green.\nUpload the number list. \nAL01 cleans, dedupes, and removes anyone on the do-not-contact list.\nSend the approved Marketing template. \nAL04 sends it, personalising the variables per contact, in rate-safe batches from the warmed number pool.\nThe guard watches quality. \nAL05 checks the rating (Meta re-evaluates every 6 hours). Yellow throttles, red stops. Blockers are suppressed instantly.\nReplies open a free 24h window. \nOnce someone replies you can converse free-form at no cost, and AL07 pushes it to LeadOS to close.\nWarm list vs cold list — the honest part\nThe API will not stop you sending to a cold list, but cold lists get blocked and reported, quality drops to red, and the number dies. Warm-ish lists (past enquiries, your students, event contacts) — send confidently. Truly cold lists — send in small batches and watch quality, or better, run them through a click-to-WhatsApp ad first to warm them up.\nB.6 Limits & cost to plan around\nWhat\nThe rule (2026)\nDaily reach (tier)\n250 unverified → 1,000 verified → 10K → 100K → unlimited, gated by quality. A big list goes out over several days.\nShared number pool\nSince Oct 2025 all numbers in one Business Portfolio share the daily limit. Extra numbers add safety, not more volume.\nFrequency cap\nA user can receive ~2 marketing messages/day across all brands. Over that, your message is blocked (error 131049) even if your account is fine.\nCost (India)\nMarketing ₹1.09/message, Utility ₹0.145, Service (replies) free. A 1,000-contact campaign ≈ ₹1,090.\nUse the Campaign Planner screen in the app to estimate cost and days before you upload a list.\nPart C — Cold email domain setup\nEmail is our cold outreach engine — but only if it is authenticated and warmed up. In 2026 Gmail, Yahoo and Microsoft reject unauthenticated mail outright, so these steps are not optional.\nC.1 Buy a separate sending domain\nRegister a domain just for outreach (e.g. getabm.in). Never send cold from abmgroups.org — one spam flag would hurt our real email.\nA slightly older, warmed domain performs better than one registered yesterday. Buy it early.\nC.2 Pick a sending provider\nUse a reputable provider so you get proper authentication and reputation: Amazon SES, Postmark, or Google Workspace on the cold domain (fine for our small volume). Avoid sending from a random SMTP.\nC.3 Set the three DNS records (required)\nRecord\nWhat it does\nNote\nSPF\nLists which servers may send for the domain\nKeep under 10 DNS lookups\nDKIM\nCryptographically signs each email\n1024-bit min, 2048 better\nDMARC\nTies SPF+DKIM together, sets the policy\nStart at p=none\nThe "From" domain must match the SPF or DKIM domain (this is "alignment") — otherwise DMARC fails even if SPF passes.\nStart DMARC at p=none, monitor for 4–6 weeks, then move to quarantine. Setting p=reject on day one breaks legitimate mail.\nAdd a valid PTR (reverse DNS) if using a dedicated IP.\nAdd one-click unsubscribe (List-Unsubscribe header) — required for marketing mail.\nVerify before sending\nSend a test to a Gmail address, open it, click "Show original" — SPF, DKIM and DMARC should all show PASS with aligned domains. Or use a free checker (mail-tester) and aim for 9+/10.\nC.4 Create the inboxes\n2–3 mailboxes on the domain. Use real-sounding names (kamar@getabm.in), not info@, sales@, or rep1@ — those get flagged as bulk.\nAdd a profile photo and signature to each so they look human.\nC.5 Warm up (3 weeks — do not skip)\nA brand-new mailbox that suddenly sends 50 cold emails looks like spam. Ramp slowly:\nWeek\nEmails / inbox / day\nSend to\nWeek 1\n~20\nWarm/known contacts first\nWeek 2\n~30\nMix in real prospects\nWeek 3+\n~50 steady\nFull outreach\nKeep the spam-complaint rate under 0.3% (aim under 0.1%). AL05 watches bounce/complaint rate and throttles if it climbs.\nSet up Google Postmaster Tools to watch your reputation.\nPart D — AI (Grok) API key\nCreate an account at the xAI developer console and generate an API key for Grok.\nSave it as the GROK_API_KEY environment variable (never hardcode it in a workflow).\nThe same key powers all four AI jobs: personalise, classify reply, draft reply, score lead.\nPart E — n8n credentials & environment\nImport the 9 workflow files, then set these credentials and environment variables once.\nE.1 Credentials to create in n8n\nPostgres — the leados_db connection (used by every workflow).\nSMTP / email provider — for AL03 (the cold domain inbox).\nThe Grok and Meta tokens are read from env, not stored as n8n credentials.\nE.2 Environment variables\n# AI\nGROK_API_KEY=\nAI_PROVIDER=grok\n \n# WhatsApp (Meta app-level; per-number IDs live in the DB)\nMETA_APP_ID=\nMETA_APP_SECRET=\nLEADOS_PHONE_ID=          # LeadOS number, for alerts only\n \n# Cold email\nCOLD_DOMAIN=getabm.in\nEMAIL_PROVIDER=ses        # ses | postmark | gsuite\nEMAIL_API_KEY=\n \n# Integrations\nDATABASE_URL=postgres://.../leados_db\nLEADOS_API=               # base URL for AI Brain sync (AL09)\nLEADOS_INBOX_WEBHOOK=     # where replies are pushed (AL07)\nPDF_SERVICE_URL=          # MoU generation (AL08)\nPart F — Webhooks\nWebhooks are how replies and uploads reach the workflows. Set three.\nWebhook\nPoints to\nSet where\nWhatsApp inbound\nAL07 (/alliance/reply)\nMeta App → WhatsApp → Configuration\nEmail inbound\nAL07 (/alliance/reply)\nEmail provider inbound / IMAP\nList upload\nAL01 (/alliance/import)\nAllianceOS Upload screen\nIn the Meta App, subscribe the webhook to the "messages" field so inbound WhatsApp replies are delivered.\nVerify the webhook with the token Meta asks for during setup.\nPart G — Approval flow & auto follow-up\nG.1 How approval works\nThe AI never sends a reply on its own. When someone replies, AL07 drafts a response and pushes it — with the detected intent — into the LeadOS inbox. A human (Kamar or Karthika) approves, edits, or rejects it there, and the deal is closed in LeadOS. AllianceOS only keeps a status (replied / interested / not-interested).\nWhere it lives once approved\nApprove = send happens from the LeadOS inbox. The conversation and closing stay in LeadOS. AllianceOS shows only the status — you never manage two inboxes.\nG.2 Auto follow-up settings\nFollow-ups are automatic and configured in the alliance_sequences table. Default cadence:\nTouch\nDay\nPurpose\n1\n0\nIntro + one ask\n2\n2\nShort bump\n3\n5\nNew angle + proof\n4\n9\nBreak-up message\nAL06 schedules the next touch automatically and stops the instant they reply or are suppressed.\nTo change timing or wording, edit alliance_sequences (days) and alliance_templates (copy) — no code change.\nFollow-ups never exceed 4 touches. That cap is deliberate — more feels like spam.\nGo-live checklist\nMeta Business verified; 2–3 fresh WhatsApp numbers added with tokens saved.\nAt least one WhatsApp template approved (and an opt-in path live: QR / click-to-WhatsApp).\nCold domain bought; SPF + DKIM + DMARC all PASS with alignment (tested).\nInboxes created with human names; warm-up started (week-1 caps).\nGrok key, Meta app, email provider all in env; n8n creds set.\nThree webhooks live and verified (WA inbound, email inbound, upload).\nA test reply lands in the LeadOS inbox with intent + AI draft.\nLeadOS number 99445 09441 confirmed NOT in the AllianceOS sender pool.\nOne line to remember\nEmail goes out cold (authenticated + warmed). WhatsApp only talks to people who opted in. Replies are approved and closed in LeadOS.
