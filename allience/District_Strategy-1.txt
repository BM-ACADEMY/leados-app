PHASE 1 TARGET GEOGRAPHY
=========================

Priority 1 Districts (Start Here — Week 1-2):
- Pondicherry: Home market. Highest conversion rate. Strong brand recognition.
- Villupuram: Largest district by area adjacent to Pondicherry. High college density.
- Cuddalore: Major industrial and educational hub. Strong BPO and manufacturing presence.

Priority 2 Districts (Expand — Week 3-4):
- Kallakurichi: Arts and Science college dense. New district, low competition.
- Thiruvannamalai: Large student population. Underserved by training institutes.
- Karaikal: Pondicherry territory. Easy logistics extension.

Priority 3 Districts (Month 2):
- Salem: Major city. MBA and Engineering hubs. Good company density.
- Coimbatore: Strong IT and manufacturing sector. Higher competition but larger market.
- Chennai: National companies for Core Talents. Start with OMR corridor.
