SECTION 1 — COLLEGE OBJECTIONS AND RESPONSES
==============================================

Q: We already have a placement cell. Why do we need you?
A: We do not replace your placement cell. We work alongside it. Your cell handles internal process and paperwork. We bring external company connections, AI and Digital skill training, and upskilling programs for students who need it. Many placement officers find this reduces their workload. We handle a segment of students who need additional skill development before they are placement-ready.

Q: What is the cost to the college?
A: Zero. The Free Model involves no cost to the college at any point. We conduct workshops, career sessions, and internship programs entirely at no charge. Students who enroll in BM Academy courses do so individually and pay only after getting placed. The college pays nothing.

Q: What about our NAAC accreditation? Does this help?
A: Yes directly. We provide: industry collaboration letter on BM TechX letterhead, student attendance records, work output documentation, and project summaries. These are structured specifically to support NAAC criteria related to industry linkages, student outcomes, and practical training. This is not a generic certificate — it is structured documentation.

Q: How many students will benefit?
A: It depends on the model chosen. Free model: all interested students can attend free workshops. We offer 2 scholarship seats per batch per academic year. Students who complete BM Academy training become eligible for Core Talents placement support. There is no cap on how many students can benefit from our programs.

Q: What if results are not good after one semester?
A: Partnership is annual and auto-renewable. If either party is not satisfied after one academic year, either can terminate with 30 days notice. There is no lock-in. No financial penalty. The college risks nothing.

Q: We tried training institutes before and it did not work.
A: The difference with BM Academy is that our curriculum updates in real time from live agency work. BM TechX manages 750+ brands right now. When we teach Meta Ads, it is from campaigns we ran last week — not from a textbook. Students who complete our training have real portfolios, not certificates.

Q: What companies will actually hire our students?
A: Core Talents currently works with digital marketing agencies, IT startups, and BPO companies across Tamil Nadu and Pondicherry. We are transparent about this. We do not promise MNC hiring. We promise that trained students from our programs have measurably better placement rates than untrained peers because they have real portfolios.

SECTION 3 — CLINIC OBJECTIONS (BM TECHX)
=========================================

Q: I already have patients. I do not need marketing.
A: That is the best time to start. When your clinic is already doing well, marketing builds a waiting list. New competitor clinics open every month. Patients who find you on Google now become your long-term base. We are not replacing anything — just making sure new patients find your clinic first, not a competitor.

Q: Marketing is expensive and does not work for clinics.
A: We managed 750+ brands across India. Dental and skin clinics are a core vertical. Average result: clinics go from 3-5 new patient enquiries per month to 25-40 in 60-90 days. If results do not show in 60 days, we extend service at no charge. Rs. 25,999 for 3 months equals approximately Rs. 8,666 per month. One new patient consultation covers that cost.

Q: I tried a digital agency before and it did not work.
A: Most digital agencies create posts and call it marketing. We are different. Our work includes: GMB optimization so patients find you on Google search, Instagram Reels with patient consent testimonials so trust is built before they call, Meta Ads targeted within 5km radius of your clinic. The combination of all three is what drives enquiries — not just posting.

Q: I do not have time to manage this.
A: That is exactly why you hire us. You do not manage anything. We handle content creation, posting, ad management, GMB updates, and monthly reporting. You get a WhatsApp update every Friday with enquiry count and performance. Your time commitment is approximately 30 minutes per month for review.
