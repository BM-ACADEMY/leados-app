# Client Onboarding SOP

**Internal operating procedure | Version 1.0**
*Product name pending — "Nera" used as placeholder throughout.*

**How to use this:** every stage has an owner, a checklist and a gate. **Do not move to the next stage until the gate is cleared.** Most onboarding failures come from skipping a gate to keep a client happy.

## Stage map

| Stage | Owner | Typical duration |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 0 · Lead capture | Sales | Same day |
| 1 · Discovery call | Sales | 20 minutes |
| 2 · Free demo | Sales + Ops | 24 hrs to build, 7 days live |
| 3 · Proposal & close | Founder / Sales | 2–5 days |
| 4 · Payment & agreement | Finance | Same day |
| 5 · Document collection | Ops | 1–2 days |
| 6 · WhatsApp API setup | Ops | 2–4 days |
| 7 · AI training | Ops | 2 days |
| 8 · Templates | Ops | 1 day + Meta approval |
| 9 · Team training | Ops | 60 minutes |
| 10 · Supervised go-live | Ops | 14 days |

## STAGE 0 — Lead capture

**Owner: Sales**

1. Log every enquiry in the CRM within 2 hours — name, business, city, source, WhatsApp number.
2. Respond within 1 hour during working hours.
3. Book the discovery call.

**Opening message (copy-paste):**

> Hi [Name], thanks for reaching out about [product]. I'd like to understand your business first before showing you anything. Do you have 20 minutes tomorrow — morning or evening?

**Gate:** discovery call booked with a date and time in the CRM.

---

## STAGE 1 — Discovery call

**Owner: Sales | 20 minutes**

**Do not demo on this call.** Only ask.

The four questions:

1. How many WhatsApp enquiries do you get in a day?
2. Who replies to them, and how fast at 9pm on a Sunday?
3. How many of last month's leads did nobody follow up with?
4. Where do you track them today — notebook, Excel, or nothing?

Question 3 is the one that closes deals. Let the silence sit.

**Record in CRM:**
- Enquiries per month
- Average customer value
- Team size
- Do they send promotional broadcasts? (decides Desk vs WhatsApp line)
- Do they need email outreach? (decides Complete)
- Existing tool, if any

**Recommend a plan on the call.** One plan, not three.

| Their situation | Recommend |
| :--- | :--- |
| Only answering enquiries, small team | Desk Lite |
| Answering, 10+ users or 2 numbers | Desk Pro |
| Also wants to send offers/campaigns | WhatsApp Starter or Growth |
| B2B, needs cold outreach | Complete Entry |
| Agency reselling to their own clients | WhatsApp Agency |

**Close of call:** ask for their price list and top questions, to build the free demo.

> To show you exactly how this works for your business, send me your price list and the 10 questions customers ask you most. I'll have a demo ready in 24 hours.

**Gate:** price list and FAQ content received. **No content, no demo.** Chasing it is Sales' job, not Ops'.

---

## STAGE 2 — Free demo

**Owner: Sales briefs, Ops builds | Build within 24 hours**

1. Ops loads the content into a demo knowledge base on the shared demo number.
2. Ops tests 10 realistic customer questions before releasing it.
3. Sales sends the demo link with instructions.

**Demo release message:**

> [Name], your demo is ready. Save this number: [demo number]. Message it as if you were your own customer — ask about prices, timings, anything. This is trained on the material you sent. It's live for 7 days.

4. Follow up **48 hours later**, not on day 7.

> Did you try it? What did it get right, and where did it fall short? I'd rather fix it than oversell it.

**Hard limits:** 200 AI replies, 7 days, then archived. Never set up a WABA at this stage.

**Gate:** the prospect has actually messaged the demo. If they haven't in 4 days, call them — an untried demo means low intent, and it's better to know now.

---

## STAGE 3 — Proposal and close

**Owner: Founder or Sales**

1. Send the proposal with **their name and validity date filled in.**
2. Never send it before the demo — it's the demo that creates the desire.
3. Walk through the "things we want you to know before you sign" section aloud. Volunteering the tier limits and ban policy closes more deals than it loses.
4. Ask for the ₹2,999 pilot, not the annual contract.

> Start with the 14-day pilot at ₹2,999. It's your number, your customers. At the end you'll know your real response time. The ₹2,999 comes off your first invoice.

**Handling "let me think about it":**

> Fair enough. What's the one thing you're unsure about? If it's the price, I'd rather move you to a smaller plan than have you sign something that doesn't fit.

**Never discount. Drop a tier instead.** This is a hard rule.

**Gate:** written confirmation of plan and term on WhatsApp. A verbal yes is not a gate.

---

## STAGE 4 — Payment and agreement

**Owner: Finance**

1. Send agreement + invoice + payment link **the same day**. Delay kills momentum.
2. Agreement must include:
   - Chosen plan, term, price, onboarding fee
   - Account-risk policy (who owns a ban)
   - Support SLA and hours
   - Rate-change clause
   - Data ownership and export rights
   - Cancellation terms
3. Payment is 100% advance. **No setup work begins before payment clears.**
4. Offline payments need a second person's approval before activation.

**Gate:** payment confirmed in the bank/Razorpay, signed agreement on file. Both, not one.

---

## STAGE 5 — Document collection

**Owner: Ops | Target: 48 hours**

Send this checklist within 1 hour of payment clearing:

> **What we need to start (please send on WhatsApp):**
> 
> **Documents**
> 1. Business PAN card
> 2. Any ONE of: GST certificate / Udyam certificate / Shop & Establishment licence / Certificate of Incorporation
> 3. Business address proof (utility bill or rental agreement)
> 4. Aadhaar or PAN of the authorised person
> 
> **WhatsApp number**
> 5. A phone number NOT currently on WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business app. We strongly recommend a fresh SIM — your personal number stays untouched.
> 6. Someone available to receive an OTP on that number
> 7. Your preferred display name (must match your business name)
> 
> **Business details**
> 8. Website link (if you have one)
> 9. Business hours, address, Google Maps link
> 10. Logo (PNG) and brand colours
> 
> **Team**
> 11. Names, emails and mobile numbers of everyone who needs a login
> 
> **Contacts**
> 12. Existing customer list (Excel/CSV) — only contacts who've consented to be messaged

**Chase schedule:** reminders on day 2, day 4, day 7. After day 7, escalate to the founder for a phone call.

**The number check is critical.** Ask explicitly: *"Is this number currently on WhatsApp?"* If yes, explain that moving it destroys chat history. This single misunderstanding causes more onboarding failures than everything else combined.

**Gate:** all documents received and the number confirmed clean.

## STAGE 6 — WhatsApp API setup

**Owner: Ops | 2–4 days**

1. Create or connect Meta Business Manager
2. Submit business verification
3. Register the number, complete OTP
4. Submit display name for approval
5. Apply for green tick if eligible (free — never charge for this)
6. Store all credentials in the encrypted secret store. **Never in WhatsApp, email or a spreadsheet.**

**Set expectations in writing on day 1 of this stage:**

> Meta's own verification takes 2–5 days and is outside our control. I'll update you at every step. Also — your new number starts limited to 1,000 messages per day. It rises to 10,000 after 2–4 weeks of clean sending. This applies to every business on WhatsApp API, not just you.

**If display name is rejected:** revise to match the registered business name exactly and resubmit. Inform the client the same day.

**Gate:** number live, display name approved.

## STAGE 7 — AI training

**Owner: Ops | 2 days**

Collect and load:

| Input | Why it matters |
| :--- | :--- |
| Services/products with prices | Core |
| 20–30 common questions with answers | **The single most important input** |
| Business hours, address, location link | Core |
| Payment terms, delivery, refund policy | Prevents wrong commitments |
| Brochures, catalogues | Depth |
| Tone: formal / friendly / Tamil-English mix | Voice |
| What the AI must NEVER say | **Do not skip this** |

That last row matters most. Ask directly: *"What should the AI never promise a customer?"* Discounts, delivery dates, warranty terms, medical or legal claims — get it in writing.

**Test before release:** 20 real questions, including three awkward ones (price negotiation, complaint, competitor comparison). Record the results.

**Gate:** the client has personally tested the AI and approved its answers in writing.

---

## STAGE 8 — Templates

**Owner: Ops | 1 day + Meta approval**

1. Write the starter templates: welcome, follow-up day 2, follow-up day 5, appointment confirmation, payment reminder
2. Submit to Meta
3. **Rejections are our problem** — rewrite and resubmit free, unlimited, and tell the client we're handling it
4. Never charge for template approvals

**Gate:** at least the welcome and follow-up templates approved.

---

## STAGE 9 — Team training

**Owner: Ops | 60 minutes, on a call, recorded**

Agenda:
1. Login and MFA setup (10 min)
2. The inbox — replying, assigning, internal notes (15 min)
3. Lead records and stages (10 min)
4. When the AI hands over, and how to take over (10 min)
5. Reading the dashboard (10 min)
6. Questions (5 min)

Send the recording afterwards. New staff will need it and you won't want to repeat this.

**Gate:** every user has logged in at least once with MFA enabled.

---

## STAGE 10 — Supervised go-live

**Owner: Ops | 14 days**

- **Days 1–3:** AI drafts, human approves before sending. Ops reviews every conversation daily.
- **Days 4–7:** AI sends automatically. Ops reviews daily, corrects the knowledge base.
- **Days 8–14:** Ops reviews every second day. Client takes over monitoring.

**Watch daily:**
- Quality rating — act immediately on Yellow
- AI accuracy — any wrong answer goes into the knowledge base same day
- Handover rate — over 40% means the knowledge base is thin
- Response time
- Messaging tier and daily quota

**Day 7 check-in message:**

> One week in. Your average response time is now [X] against [Y] before. The AI has handled [N] conversations. Anything it got wrong that I should fix?

**Gate:** quality rating Green, AI accuracy acceptable to the client, no unresolved complaints.

## STAGE 11 — 30-day review

**Owner: Founder or Sales | 45 minutes**

Bring their actual numbers:

| Metric | Before | Now |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Average response time | | |
| Enquiries captured | | |
| Follow-ups sent | | |
| Leads converted | | |
| After-hours enquiries answered | | |

Three things to do in this meeting:
1. **Ask for the testimonial** — this is when they're most impressed
2. **Ask for two referrals** — "who else do you know with the same problem?"
3. **Plant the upgrade** — if they're on Desk, show them their unused contact list. Don't pitch; just show them the number of past customers they've never messaged again.

Then repeat at day 90. Reviews are the cheapest churn prevention you have.

## Common failure points

| Failure | Prevention |
| :--- | :--- |
| Client's number already on WhatsApp | Ask explicitly at Stage 5. Explain history loss |
| Documents never arrive | Chase on day 2, 4, 7. Founder call after 7 |
| Display name rejected | Match registered business name exactly |
| Client blames us for Meta tier limits | Set expectations in writing at Stage 6 |
| AI gives wrong answers | Never skip the "what must it never say" question |
| Client never turns up for training | The onboarding fee exists precisely to prevent this |
| Client sends to a non-consented list, gets restricted | Warn at Stage 5. Responsibility clause in the agreement |
| Onboarding stalls for weeks | Every stage has an owner and a date. Stalled cases go on the ops dashboard |

---

## Escalation

| Trigger | Escalate to | Within |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Stage stalled 3+ days past target | Ops lead | Same day |
| Quality rating Yellow | Founder | Same day |
| Number restricted or banned | Founder | Immediately |
| Client asks for a refund | Founder | Immediately |
| Client requests a discount | Founder — **never negotiated by Sales** | Before responding |
| SLA breach | Founder | Same day |

**The rule behind all of this**

Every stage has an owner, a **date**, and a **gate**. If any of the three is missing, the onboarding will stall — and a stalled onboarding is the single strongest predictor of a client who churns in month three.

Until Phase 9 autopilot is built, this runs manually on a shared tracker. Review the tracker every morning at 10am. Every active onboarding case gets 60 seconds.
