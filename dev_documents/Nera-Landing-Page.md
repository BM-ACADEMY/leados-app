## FAQ

**Can I use my existing WhatsApp number?**
Only if it isn't currently active on the WhatsApp app. Moving it means losing that chat history. Most clients use a fresh SIM and keep their existing number as it is.

**What documents do I need?**
Business PAN, any one business registration document (GST / Udyam / Shop & Establishment / Incorporation), address proof, and ID of the authorised person.

**Will the AI say something wrong?**
Supervised for the first two weeks. It hands over when unsure. You can switch it off any time.

**AiSensy starts at ₹999. Why is this more?**
That's software only — message charges are separate and the AI chatbot is an add-on. Add it up for your volume. We'll do that comparison with you openly.

**Do I need DLT registration like SMS?**
No. WhatsApp API sits outside TRAI's DLT framework entirely.

**What if I use up my messages mid-month?**
Buy a top-up pack, credited instantly. You're alerted at 80%, and nothing is charged without your approval. If credits run out, conversations route to your team — the AI never goes silent on a customer.

**Can I cancel?**
30 days' notice. Unused prepaid months refunded. You export all your data. We don't hold your customer list.

## BUILD NOTES
**Page order matters.** Problem before solution, price before comparison, honesty section before the final CTA. Don't move pricing to the top.

**The calculator is the key element.** Build the four-question block as an interactive widget: enquiries per month × missed follow-ups × customer value = what they lost. Show the result, then the demo button. Highest-converting element on the page.

**Primary CTA is the free demo, not the pilot.** Zero friction to start the conversation. The pilot is sold on the call, once they've seen the demo working.

**Keep the "honest part" section.** A designer will want to cut it. It is what builds trust with an SMB owner who has been burned before, and it protects you when a client hits a Meta tier limit.

**Geography in the hero.** "Tamil Nadu and Puducherry" is a feature, not a limitation — it signals local support and Tamil language, which the national players can't match.

**Every comparison claim must stay verifiable.** If a competitor changes pricing, update the table.
