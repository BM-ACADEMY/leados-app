## Things we want you to know before you sign
We'd rather tell you now than have you discover it later.

**Your new number starts with a daily limit.** Meta caps every new WhatsApp API account at 1,000 business-initiated messages per 24 hours, rising to 10,000 after roughly 2–4 weeks of clean sending. So a plan with 3,000 promotional messages cannot all be sent on day one. This is Meta's rule for everyone. No provider can bypass it.

**Numbers can be restricted.** If customers block or report your messages, Meta lowers your quality rating and can restrict the number.

| Situation | Who handles it |
| :--- | :--- |
| A template is rejected | **Us** — rewritten and resubmitted, free, unlimited |
| Quality rating drops to Yellow | **Us** — we alert you within 24 hours and advise |
| Number restricted after messaging people who never opted in | **You** — it's your contact list. We help you appeal |
| Number permanently banned | Replacement provisioned within 48 hours; you pay only Meta's costs |
| Platform down or our mistake | **Us** — service credit for the affected days |

Subscription is not refunded for a ban caused by contact lists or message content you insisted on. We will always warn you first.

**No DLT registration needed.** WhatsApp API sits outside TRAI's DLT framework, unlike SMS marketing.
