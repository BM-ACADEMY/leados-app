Lead OS

AI-Powered Google Business Profile Management Platform

Lead OS is an AI-powered platform that helps businesses and agencies manage Google Business Profiles, Google Reviews, Google Posts, Business Information, Business Insights, Multi-location listings, and Local SEO from one centralized dashboard.

Lead OS uses official Google OAuth 2.0 to securely connect to your Google account without ever storing your password. Access tokens are encrypted using AES-256 and transmitted via TLS 1.3.

  • Manage Google Reviews and auto-respond with AI
  • Publish and schedule Google Posts
  • Track Business Insights (calls, views, directions)
  • Manage multiple Google Business Profile locations
  • Improve Local SEO and Google Map Pack rankings
