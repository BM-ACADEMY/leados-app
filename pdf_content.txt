1.  Executive Summary  
 
Google Thedal is the organic search intelligence module inside the LeadOS platform, built for BM TechX to 
deliver SEO -as-a-service to Tamil Nadu SMEs. While GMB Mafia controls Google Maps rankings, Thedal controls 
organic web search rankings — together for ming a full Google domination stack.  
 
Thedal = Search (Tamil)  
When a business in Pondicherry or Tamil Nadu searches for their name, competitor, or service on Google — 
Thedal makes sure our clients appear first. We find them before they find us.  
This is not a reporting tool. It is an automated SEO engine that tracks, alerts, generates content, audits, and 
delivers branded PDF reports — all without manual work.  
 
STRATEGIC POSITION  
Problem it solves  Tamil Nadu SMEs spend on ads but have zero visibility into why competitors 
outrank them organically on Google  
Solution  Automated daily rank tracking + GSC data + AI content + technical audit + 
monthly branded PDF report — all in one LeadOS module  
Who pays for it  BM TechX charges clients ₹3,999 –₹11,999/month. They get a full SEO team 
in a dashboard.

2.  What Thedal Does — Feature Map  
 
# Feature  What It Does  Automation  
1 Keyword Rank Tracking  Daily rank check for every keyword via ValueSERP. 
Tracks position 1 –100, rank change, SERP features 
(featured snippet, PAA, local pack)  n8n WF1 — 6 AM daily  
2 GSC Intel  Pulls Google Search Console data: impressions, clicks, 
CTR, avg position, top queries, top pages, index 
coverage  n8n WF2 — Monday 5 AM

page speed, image alt, schema, mobile -friendliness, 
robots.txt, sitemap, thin content, canonicals, broken 
links n8n WF3 — Sunday 2 AM  
4 Content Factory  AI-generated blog posts (English/Tamil/Tanglish), 
meta tag rewrites, topic calendar ideas, and JSON -LD 
schema markup via GPT -4o or Grok 3  On-demand in UI  
5 Monthly PDF Report  Branded PDF with rank data, GSC metrics, audit 
score, top wins — auto -generated on 1st of every 
month for every client  n8n WF4 — 1st of month  
6 Rank Drop Alert  WhatsApp alert to Kamar when any keyword drops > 
5 positions or enters Top 10  n8n WF6 — after WF1  
7 Client Onboard  First rank scan + first audit fires automatically when 
new client is added  n8n WF5 — webhook  
8 SERP Radar  Live dashboard widget showing all clients' rank 
health at a glance  Real -time in UI  
9 Gap Hunter  AI keyword opportunities — 15 high -value keywords
